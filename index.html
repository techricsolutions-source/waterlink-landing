What you get

Live readings
Flow rate and total volume for every meter, in one dashboard you can open on a phone.
Burst and leak alerts
Night-flow checks and meter alarms raise an alert as soon as a meter reports, so a leak does not run for weeks.
Remote valve control
Close or open a valve from the dashboard. The command reaches the meter at its next report, and the dashboard shows what the meter confirms.
Billing and compliance
Built for Kenyan rules: M-Pesa payment tracking, KRA eTIMS invoices and Water Resources Authority reporting.

How it works

  1. The meter measuresAn ultrasonic meter with a built-in valve reads flow with no moving parts, then reports over the NB-IoT mobile network.
  2. The platform checksWaterLink validates each reading, stores it, and raises alerts for leaks, low batteries, tampering and valve faults.
  3. You decideSee usage, resolve alerts, send bills and control valves. Each client sees only their own meters.

Who it is for

Water utilities that need to cut losses, gated estates that bill residents fairly, and borehole owners who sell water and want every litre accounted for. We are onboarding pilot customers now.
